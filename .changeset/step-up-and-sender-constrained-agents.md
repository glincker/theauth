Step-up authentication (RFC 9470) and sender constrained agent tokens (RFC 7800, RFC 9449, RFC 8705). Sessions record how the user proved who they are (`session.auth` with `acr`, `amr` and `authTime`, and `sessions.recordAuth` for a step-up). Approvals take a rule (`approval.requireAcr`, `maxAuthAge`, `stepUp`, or `stepUp` on `request()`) and `approve` throws `StepUpRequiredError` unless the approver's assertion meets it; the admin API answers 401 with the `insufficient_user_authentication` challenge, and risk tiers can name the rule. The MCP server honors `acr_values` and `max_age` when given `resolveAuthContext`, signs `acr`, `amr` and `auth_time` into tokens, reports them from introspection, and `requireMcpAuth` and `requireScopes` can demand a login strength. Agent tokens can be bound with `cnf` (DPoP key or certificate thumbprint), checked on `POST /authorize/token`, `requireAgent` and inside `authorizeByToken`, with a replay cache, nonces, `POST /agents/introspect` and `agents.confirmation.required`. Marked major because `DecisionCode` gains `confirmation_required`, `confirmation_failed` and `approval_step_up_insufficient`, `SessionManager` gains `recordAuth`, and a `step_up` risk tier can now be satisfied by a strong enough approval. Migrations 0009 and 0011 add nullable columns. See `docs/step-up-auth.mdx`, `docs/dpop-agent-tokens.mdx` and `docs/upgrading-to-2.mdx`.
